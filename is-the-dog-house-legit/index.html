How Fairness Works

The Dog House uses a certified Random Number Generator (RNG): every spin is independent and cannot be predicted or influenced by past results. Pragmatic Play's games are tested by Gaming Laboratories International (GLI) and carry the "Gaming Labs Certified" mark, and the studio is licensed by the Malta Gaming Authority (MGA/B2B/317/2016) and the UK Gambling Commission, among other regulators. RTP (Return to Player) is a long-run statistical average measured over millions of spins - it is not a promise for any single session. Short-term results vary widely.

Is The Dog House Rigged?

No. Because the game runs on certified RNG, no one, including the operator, can alter a spin's outcome. It does have a house edge, like every casino game: the Megaways RTP is 96.55%, meaning around 3.45% is retained by the house over the long run. That edge is normal and disclosed, not manipulation. What people often call "rigged" is simply variance: random runs of losses that feel unfair but aren't.

Why Predictor / Hack Tools Are Scams

No software can predict or influence RNG outcomes. "Predictor", "signal", "hack" and "best time to play" products are scams and can carry malware. Never pay for or install them.
